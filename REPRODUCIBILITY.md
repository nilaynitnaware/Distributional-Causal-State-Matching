# Reproduction index

Run commands from the repository root. `OUT` must be absent or empty; add
`--overwrite` only when intentionally reusing it. `DATA` denotes user-supplied
publisher data or an omitted historical bank with the layout documented by the
runner. Long or historically unbounded regeneration also requires `--yes`.
For parallel regenerations, `--workers N` is available for e and k--o.  The
defaults preserve the documented historical launch counts: 4 for e's parallel
n=40 sub-run and 8 for the k--o A--F campaign.  Each task has a fixed derived
seed, so worker count affects scheduling only, not task inputs.

## What is not included and why

- Complete n=20 and n=40 tree banks (89 MiB and 392 MiB locally) and broad raw
  output trees are omitted because they are large and regenerable. Other
  omitted archives include exact population 4.7 MiB, Small-MEC 27 MiB, Stage B
  468 MiB, calibration-free 51 MiB, and robustness 141 MiB. Compact summaries
  and checksums are included instead.
- Raw Causal Chambers / Light Tunnel data are not included. They were obtained
  from the original publisher and are not ours to license. Physical commands
  therefore require `--data-root`.
- Historical byte-exact source linkage is unavailable for Stage B,
  calibration-free, robustness, and Mk1. See the
  [environment record](docs/reproducibility/ENVIRONMENT.md) and
  [execution provenance](docs/reproducibility/execution-provenance.md).
